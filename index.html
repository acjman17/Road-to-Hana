🌺 Road to Hana

A free, narrated drive from Pāʻia to Kīpahulu. Your passenger taps play as you pass each mile marker.
Open this page while you still have signal. It saves itself (about 15 MB) so the narration, music and GPS work in dead zones.

Driving etiquette (read before you go)

Most of the 59 bridges are one lane. The rule is to yield as a group: if 5 or 6 cars are already crossing from the other side, wait for them all, then go. Wave thanks.

If a local is behind you, pull over at the next safe spot and let them by. People live and work on this road.

Only park in marked spots, never on a bridge or a blind curve. "Kapu" and "Keep Out" signs mean private land. Respect them.

Fill up on gas in Pāʻia. There is very little fuel until Hāna, and it's expensive there.
